Phlegopsis erythroptera

(Rotspiegel-Ameisenvogel)

The reddish-winged bare-eye (Phlegopsis erythroptera) is a species of insectivorous passerine bird in subfamily Thamnophilinae of family Thamnophilidae, the "typical antbirds". It is found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, and Venezuela.

The reddish-winged bare-eye was described by the English bird artist and ornithologist John Gould in 1855 and given the binomial name Formicarius erythroptera. It has two subspecies, the nominate P. e. erythroptera (Gould, 1855) and P. e. ustulata (Todd, 1927).
